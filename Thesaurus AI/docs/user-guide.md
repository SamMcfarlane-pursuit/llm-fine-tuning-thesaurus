## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Visit the Thesaurus AI LLM Fine-Tuning platform at [yourdomain.com](https://yourdomain.com)
2. Click on the "Sign Up" button in the top-right corner
3. Choose your preferred sign-up method:
   - Email and password
   - GitHub
   - Gmail
4. Follow the prompts to complete the registration process
5. Verify your email address if required

### Logging In

1. Click on the "Log In" button in the top-right corner
2. Enter your credentials or choose your preferred sign-in method
3. You will be redirected to the dashboard after successful login

### Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides an overview of your learning journey, including:

- Your progress in workshops and tutorials
- Recommended next steps
- Recent achievements
- Learning streak information
- Bookmarked content

## Visual Thesaurus

The Visual Thesaurus is an interactive tool that helps you explore relationships between LLM fine-tuning concepts.

### Using the Visual Thesaurus

1. Navigate to the "Explore" section
2. The Visual Thesaurus will load with a central concept
3. Click on any concept to see its definition and related terms
4. Double-click on a concept to make it the central node
5. Use the zoom controls to zoom in and out
6. Use the search bar to find specific concepts

### Understanding Relationships

The Visual Thesaurus uses different colors and line styles to represent different types of relationships:

- **Solid lines**: Direct relationships
- **Dashed lines**: Indirect relationships
- **Green nodes**: Foundational concepts
- **Blue nodes**: Intermediate concepts
- **Purple nodes**: Advanced concepts

### Searching the Visual Thesaurus

1. Enter a term or phrase in the search bar
2. The thesaurus will highlight matching concepts
3. Click on a search result to navigate to that concept
4. Use the "Clear" button to reset the search

## Workshops

Workshops provide hands-on learning experiences with step-by-step guides for implementing LLM fine-tuning techniques.

### Workshop Structure

Each workshop is divided into modules, and each module contains:

- **Introduction**: Overview of the module
- **Theory**: Explanation of the concepts
- **Practice**: Hands-on exercises
- **Quiz**: Test your knowledge
- **Resources**: Additional reading and references

### Completing Workshops

To complete a workshop:

1. Navigate to the "Workshops" section
2. Choose a workshop from the list
3. Start with the first module
4. Read the theory and complete the exercises
5. Take the quiz at the end of each module
6. Mark the module as complete
7. Move on to the next module

### Interactive Exercises

Interactive exercises allow you to practice coding directly in the browser:

1. Read the exercise instructions
2. Edit the code in the provided editor
3. Click "Run" to execute the code
4. View the output and debug if necessary
5. Click "Reset" to start over
6. Click "Show Solution" if you need help

## Google Colab Integration

The platform integrates with Google Colab for running notebooks in a cloud environment.

### Opening Notebooks in Colab

To open a notebook in Google Colab:

1. Navigate to a workshop or tutorial with a Colab notebook
2. Click on the "Open in Colab" button
3. Sign in to your Google account if prompted
4. The notebook will open in Google Colab
5. Follow the instructions in the notebook

### Saving Your Work

To save your work in Google Colab:

1. Click on "File" in the Colab menu
2. Choose "Save a copy in Drive"
3. The notebook will be saved to your Google Drive
4. You can access it later from your Google Drive

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Login Issues

- **Issue**: Unable to log in
- **Solution**:
  - Check your username and password
  - Reset your password if needed
  - Clear your browser cache and cookies
  - Try a different browser

#### Content Not Loading

- **Issue**: Content not loading properly
- **Solution**:
  - Refresh the page
  - Clear your browser cache
  - Check your internet connection
  - Try a different browser

#### Interactive Exercises Not Working

- **Issue**: Interactive exercises not working
- **Solution**:
  - Check for JavaScript errors in the console
  - Ensure your browser is up to date
  - Try a different browser
  - Disable browser extensions that might interfere

#### Progress Not Saving

- **Issue**: Progress not being saved
- **Solution**:
  - Ensure you are logged in
  - Complete the entire module before marking as complete
  - Check your internet connection
  - Contact support if the issue persists

### Getting Help

If you encounter any issues not covered in this guide:

1. Check the FAQ section
2. Use the Help button in the bottom-right corner
3. Contact support at support@yourdomain.com
4. Join the community forum for peer support
