TensorFlow Exercises

Hands-on exercises to master TensorFlow through practical implementation of machine learning models and techniques.

Getting Started

These exercises are designed to help you build practical skills with TensorFlow. Each exercise includes:

  • Clear learning objectives
  • Step-by-step instructions
  • Code templates to get you started
  • Challenges to extend your learning
  • Solutions for reference

To get the most out of these exercises, we recommend:

  • Completing the exercises in order, as they build on each other
  • Trying to solve the exercises on your own before looking at the solutions
  • Experimenting with the code to deepen your understanding
  • Taking on the challenges to test your skills
